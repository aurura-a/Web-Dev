Pancakes

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Flour 1 cup
Milk 1 cup
Egg 1

Details

  • Preparation time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: 10 minutes
  • Servings: 2

Steps

  1. Mix flour, milk, and egg.
  2. Heat a pan.
  3. Pour batter and cook both sides.
